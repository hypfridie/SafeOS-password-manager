# Privacy Policy for SafeOS – Password Manager

**Last updated:** January 2026

SafeOS (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how **SafeOS – Password Manager** handles user data.

---

## Information We Collect

SafeOS is a privacy-focused application.

- We **do not collect** personal information such as name, email address, or phone number.
- All passwords and sensitive data are stored **locally on your device**.
- We do not operate any servers and do not receive your data.

---

## Password Storage & Security

- All stored passwords are **encrypted** on your device.
- Access to the app is protected using:
  - PIN code
  - Biometric authentication (fingerprint or face unlock)
- Auto-lock prevents unauthorized access.

Your data remains under **your control at all times**.

---

## Backup & Restore

- SafeOS offers **optional Google Drive backup**.
- Backups are encrypted and stored in **your own Google Drive account**.
- We cannot access, view, or modify your backups.

---

## Data Sharing

SafeOS **does not share data** with:
- Advertisers
- Analytics providers
- Third-party services

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data.

---

## Permissions

SafeOS may request the following permissions:
- **Biometric authentication** – for secure app access
- **Storage access** – for importing/exporting backups
- **Internet access** – only for Google Drive backup (if enabled)

Permissions are used strictly for app functionality.

---

## Children’s Privacy

SafeOS is not intended for children under the age of 13.  
We do not knowingly collect any data from children.
